About Me

As a Computer Engineering student at HCMUT, I am deeply passionate about the intersection of hardware and software. I am a highly disciplined and responsible engineer who approaches every project with a continuous desire to learn, adapt, and refine my craft. My goal is to build reliable, scalable, and impactful technology solutions.

Embedded Systems Engineering: I am captivated by hardware architecture and low-level programming. My expertise spans C/C++, Altium Designer (schematic/PCB), and microcontroller development (STM32, ESP32). I am committed to rigorous engineering standards, actively applying the V-Model process, conducting unit tests, and exploring advanced concepts like the AUTOSAR architecture and edge-based computer vision deployment.

Full-Stack Web Development: I love translating complex requirements into seamless digital experiences. I specialize in building robust system architectures (such as the MVC pattern) and scalable backend APIs. My technical stack includes Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, PHP, JavaScript, and Bootstrap, supported by strong skills in database design (DDL/DML) and team leadership.

I am always eager to bring my strong sense of ownership and multidisciplinary skill set to a collaborative, forward-thinking team.
